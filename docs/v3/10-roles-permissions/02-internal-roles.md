# Internal Roles

Two internal roles exist that are not assignable through the standard UI, and one licence-bound role:

- **Component Manager** (`component_manager`) — system role for managing the company-wide component library. Unlocks the Component Manager menu section.
- **Legal Manager** (`legal_manager`) — system role for legal document and compliance tracking. Unlocks the Legal Manager menu section.

- **CVD Coordinator** (`cvd_coordinator`) — requires the `cvdself` license. It is assignable only in companies that hold the license, carries no permissions in the app and is passed on to the CVD service as a role claim.

The internal roles are secured: they are not offered in the role selection.
