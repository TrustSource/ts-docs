# Getting Support

The right bar gives you two ways to get help in ***TrustSource***.

📸 *Screenshot: the right bar with the Help icon and the Contact Account Admin icon outlined.*

| Right bar icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Help** (question mark) | Opens the ***TrustSource*** support center at support.trustsource.io in a new tab, with the knowledge base and the way to open a ticket. On small screens the same link is called **Support** in the menu. |
| **Contact Account Admin** (paper plane) | Opens a new email in your mail program, addressed to your company's **Distribution email** if one is set under [Company details](../06-administration/02a-company-details.md), otherwise to the first account admin (or, without one, the first manager) of your company. The icon is hidden when no address is found. |

Ask your account admin for access, roles and company settings. For problems with ***TrustSource*** itself, see [Contact Support](../11-troubleshooting-faq/06-contact-support.md).
