# Legal Manager

> [!NOTE]
> **Required role:** `legal_manager`

The **Legal mngr.** menu group is visible only to users with the `legal_manager` role. It holds the legal reference data that ***TrustSource*** uses to assess licenses: the license definitions with their legal constraints, preset legal-requirement templates, and the list of copyrights and trademarks.

| Menu entry | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Manage Legal** | The license definitions: add licenses, edit their text and legal constraints. See [Manage Legal](01-manage-legal.md). |
| **Legal templates** | System-wide templates for the legal requirements of projects and modules. See [Legal Templates](02-legal-templates.md). |
| **Copyright & Trademarks** | The list of copyrights and trademarks shown on the public Copyright & Trademarks page. See [Copyright & Trademarks](03-copyright-trademarks.md). |

Everyone can read the license texts under [Knowledge → Licenses](../05-knowledge/06-licenses.md).
