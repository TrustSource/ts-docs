# Mitigations & Status

> [!NOTE]
> **Required license feature:** `threat`

Countermeasures for a threat are proposed by the
[Threat Modeling Agent](../../../13-capabilities/07-threat-model-agent.md)
during its *Derive Countermeasures* step — typically one to three per
threat, each with a standards reference, an estimated risk reduction
and a validation method. Once a person marks a countermeasure as
completed or verified, subsequent agent runs leave it alone.

This page describes the list where you review those countermeasures,
assign owners, and track their status over time.
