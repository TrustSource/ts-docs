# Tab: Photos

> [!NOTE]
> **Required license feature:** `products`
> **Editing:** `developer`, `compliance_manager`, `company_security_manager` or `account_admin`

The **Photos** tab is a gallery of pictures and illustrations of the product, useful for identification and regulatory documentation.

## Upload a picture

1. Click **Upload additional pictures or illustrations**.
2. Select an image. Accepted formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, AVIF, SVG, HEIC and HEIF.

The upload starts immediately (the button shows *Updating product* meanwhile) and the picture appears in the gallery. Other file types are rejected with "Invalid file type. Please upload a valid image."

## Browse and remove

Use the gallery to page through the pictures: click a picture, or focus the gallery and use the left and right arrow keys, to bring it to the front. To delete the picture in front, click **Remove picture**; it is removed at once, without a confirmation. The button is visible once at least one picture exists.

Each picture stores who uploaded it and when. Photos can be downloaded together with the product documents; see [Documents](06-tab-documents.md).
