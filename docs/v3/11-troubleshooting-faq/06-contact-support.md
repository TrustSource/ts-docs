# Contact Support

**Email:** [support@trustsource.io](mailto:support@trustsource.io)

**Support center:** [support.trustsource.io](https://support.trustsource.io/hc/en-us), also reachable with the **Help** icon in the right bar.

When opening a ticket, please include:

1. Your **company name** and **account type** (shown in brackets after the company name at the top of the left navigation).
2. The **page or feature** where the issue occurs.
3. **Steps to reproduce** the problem.
4. Any **error messages** (screenshot or text).
5. Your **browser** and **OS** (for UI issues), and the ***TrustSource*** version shown at the bottom of the left navigation.

For urgent security issues, use the subject line "SECURITY" to trigger priority handling.
