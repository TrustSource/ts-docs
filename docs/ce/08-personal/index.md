# Your Personal Area

These pages cover what every signed-in user of ***TrustSource CE*** has, whatever their roles: updates, messages, profile, help and the API documentation. Most of them open from the right bar.

| Page | What it covers | Opened from |
|---|---|---|
| [Updates](01-updates.md) | Release notes shipped with your installation. | Bell icon **Updates** |
| [Messages](02-inbox-messages.md) | Notifications in the folders Inbox, Marked and Trash. | Envelope icon **Messages** |
| [Profile](03-profile.md) | Name, theme, password, two-factor authentication, personal access keys, notification preferences. | Your name and roles in the header |
| [Help and Contact](04-support.md) | Help link, contacting your Account admin, reporting problems. | **Help** and **Contact Account Admin** icons |
| [API Documentation](05-api-documentation.md) | The built-in Swagger UI for the REST API. | **API documentation** icon |

On small screens these entries move into the left navigation as **Updates**, **Inbox**, **Profile**, **Contact Account Admin**, **Support**, **API Documentation** and **Logout**.
