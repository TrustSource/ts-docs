# Outbound

> [!NOTE]
> **Required role:** the **Outbound** menu is shown to Compliance manager, Manager and Account admin.

This chapter covers the documents ***TrustSource CE*** produces for people outside your team: bills of materials, attribution files, security advisories, and the tool that checks whether a document really came from your installation.

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [SBOM + CBOM](01-sbom-cbom.md) | Create, edit, version, freeze and download bills of materials (CycloneDX SBOM, CBOM, AIBOM; SPDX; PDF). |
| [Notice Files](02-notice-files.md) | Attribution files: create, clear, version, freeze and download (PDF, TXT). |
| [CSAF](04-csaf.md) | Create, import and edit CSAF advisories and VEX documents, publish them to a CSAF provider, share them through feed keys. |
| [Verify](05-verify.md) | Check the signature of a document served by your installation. |

> [!TIP]
> An approval creates the notice file and the SBOM of a release automatically. See the [approval workflow](../03-internal/06-approvals/index.md). You can also create them on demand for any project or module.

SOUP lists and CVD cases are not part of TrustSource CE. See [CE and SaaS compared](../13-ce-vs-saas.md).
