# Legal Templates

> [!NOTE]
> **Required role:** Account admin to create, edit and remove templates. Compliance manager can open the list and the templates.

Legal templates are presets for the **legal requirements** of a project or module in ***TrustSource CE***: how it is sold, operated and distributed, and what intellectual-property protection it needs. They belong to your company. Maintain them under **Administration → Settings → Legal templates**; the page is titled **Company legal settings templates**. When you set up the legal settings of a project or module, **Import settings from Template** copies a template's answers; see [Project Settings → Legal](../03-internal/01-projects/settings/02-legal.md) and [Module Settings → Legal](../03-internal/02-modules/settings/02-legal.md).

## The template list

The list shows each template's **order**, **Type** (*project* or *module*) and **Name**. Select a row to open it; **Remove** deletes a template after a confirmation.

To add one, press **Create template** and enter a **Name**, choose the **Type** (*Project* or *Module*) and optionally an **Order**; then press **Create Rule**.

## Edit a template

Under **Update template information** you change name and order and save with **Update template**. Below, the template's requirements are set in one of two ways:

- **Start wizard** guides you through a questionnaire (**Legal settings wizard**) and ends with **Finish**;
- **View all** shows all questions in one form.

Press **Update legal requirements** to save. For a **Project** template the questions cover the *Commercial Aspects* (if unsure, choose *usage fee*, the most restrictive), the *Operating Model* (if unsure, choose the *embedded model*) and *Intellectual Property Aspects* (inbound protection against contributor claims and outbound protection when you defend your rights). For a **Module** template they cover *Trade Secret Aspects*, the *Licensing Strategy* and the *Distribution of the module*.

Creating, changing and removing templates is written to the company's audit log.
